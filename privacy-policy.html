Turbosnail Amazon Patrol is an internal seller-operations application. It accesses Amazon data only after the Amazon account owner grants permission. It does not sell Amazon account data or use it for advertising unrelated to the authorized store.

1. Scope and data controller

This Privacy Notice applies to the Turbosnail Amazon Patrol application, dashboard, scheduled patrol jobs, and related server endpoints (together, the "Application"). The Application is operated by the Turbosnail Amazon Patrol project owner and is used only for authorized Amazon seller and advertising accounts.

The Application is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Amazon. Amazon, Amazon Ads, and Selling Partner API are trademarks of Amazon.com, Inc. or its affiliates.

2. Data we access

After authorization, the Application may access business data made available through Amazon Selling Partner API, Amazon Ads API, and related Amazon services.

Authorization data
Seller identifiers, marketplace identifiers, advertising profile identifiers, application client identifiers, and OAuth tokens needed to maintain the authorized connection.
Commerce data
Orders, order status, fulfillment and shipping deadlines, inventory, listings, product identifiers, pricing, sales, traffic, returns, refunds, and finance-related metrics.
Advertising data
Advertising profiles, campaigns, impressions, clicks, spend, attributed sales or purchases, ACOS, and other aggregate advertising performance metrics.
Operational data
Patrol run results, source health, timestamps, error messages, generated alerts, dashboard sessions, and configuration status.

The Application does not request Amazon account passwords or payment card numbers. It does not intentionally use buyer personal information for marketing. Order-related fields are processed only when needed for fulfillment, risk, finance, or reporting purposes authorized by the account owner.

3. How data is used

  • Build the seller dashboard and show store performance for the authorized marketplaces.
  • Detect fulfillment, inventory, listing, pricing, advertising, and storefront risks.
  • Generate operational alerts and AI-assisted patrol summaries.
  • Maintain audit records, troubleshoot API failures, and improve reliability.
  • Comply with applicable legal, tax, accounting, and security obligations.

The Application relies on the account owner's authorization and the legitimate business need to operate the authorized store. It does not use Amazon data to make automated decisions about consumers or to build consumer profiles.

4. Service providers and disclosure

Data may be processed by service providers that host or operate the Application, subject to their applicable terms and security measures. These providers may include:

  • Amazon services used to retrieve authorized business data.
  • Supabase or a comparable database provider for storage.
  • Vercel or a comparable application hosting provider.
  • A configured AI model provider that receives limited operational metrics and alert information to generate a patrol briefing.
  • A configured notification provider, such as Feishu, Slack, or Telegram, when alert delivery is enabled.

We do not sell Amazon account data. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect the Application from security threats, or as part of a transfer of the authorized business operation, subject to appropriate protections.

5. Retention and security

Business metrics, alerts, AI briefings, and operational logs are retained for as long as needed to support the authorized store, meet reporting obligations, resolve incidents, and maintain historical trends. Authorization tokens remain active until they expire, are replaced, or are revoked.

Credentials are intended to be stored in server-side environment variables or protected server storage and are not exposed to browser code. The Application uses authenticated or restricted access, HTTPS for network communication, and reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

The Amazon account owner can revoke the Application's access through the applicable Amazon authorization management page or by contacting the Application operator. Revoking authorization stops future API access, although previously generated business records may remain subject to retention obligations.

Authorized users may request access, correction, export, or deletion of Application data by contacting the operator. Requests may be limited where the data is needed for legal compliance, security, dispute resolution, or the operation of the authorized account.

7. Cookies and tracking

This privacy page does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party cookies. The Application dashboard may use a necessary, server-set HttpOnly session cookie to authenticate authorized dashboard users. The Application does not use Amazon data for cross-site behavioral advertising.

8. Contact and changes

Privacy questions and data requests can be directed to the project owner through the public contact method listed on github.com/manyi100. Please reference "Turbosnail Amazon Patrol privacy request". Do not include Amazon passwords, client secrets, or refresh tokens in a public message.

This Privacy Notice may be updated when the Application's data practices or service providers change. The updated date will be shown at the top of this page. Material changes will apply after the revised notice is published.

This page is provided for transparency and Amazon authorization consent. It is not a representation that the Application is available to the general public.
